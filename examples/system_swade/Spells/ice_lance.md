# Ice Lance

*Arcane Power · Novice*

| Power Points | Range    | Duration | Damage |
| :----------: | :------: | :------: | :----: |
|     1–6      | 12/24/48 | Instant  |  2d6   |

## Effect

Conjures a spear of jagged ice and hurls it at a single target.
Make an **Arcane Skill** roll against the target's **Parry** (if in melee) or **4** (ranged).

* **Success:** 2d6 cold damage.
* **Raise:** +1d6 damage and the target is **Distracted** until the end of their next turn.

## Power Modifiers

| Modifier         | PP Cost | Description                                       |
| :--------------  | :-----: | :-----------------------------------------------  |
| Additional Lance |   +1    | Conjure one extra lance per +1 PP (max 3 lances). |
| Heavy Weapon     |   +1    | Damage counts as a Heavy Weapon.                  |
| Seek             |   +1    | Reroll one miss; take the second result.          |

## Notes

Each lance is an independent projectile requiring its own Arcane Skill roll.
On a raise, shards of ice scatter — targets within 1" of the primary target make an **Agility** roll or suffer 1d4 cold damage.
