# Shadowstep Blade

*Magic Weapon · Dagger · Requires Attunement*

| Damage          | Range    | Weight | Properties                     |
| :-------------: | :------: | :----: | :----------------------------: |
| 1d4+1 piercing  | 20/60 ft |  1 lb  | Finesse, Light, Thrown         |

## Attunement Bonus

While attuned, you gain **+1 to attack and damage rolls** made with this weapon.

## Properties

* **Shadow Step (1/Short Rest):** As a bonus action, teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space in dim light or darkness you can see. Until the start of your next turn, you have advantage on the first attack you make.
* **Shadow Shroud:** While in dim light or darkness, you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

## Sentience

The blade has **Charisma 14** and communicates through emotions and brief mental impressions.
It craves darkness and resents being used in direct sunlight.

* **Restless in Light:** If you spend more than 1 hour in direct sunlight without entering dim light or darkness, the blade grows irritable. Make a **DC 12 Charisma saving throw** or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls until you find shade.
* **Whisper of Warning:** Once per day, the blade alerts its attuned wielder to unseen danger; you cannot be surprised.
