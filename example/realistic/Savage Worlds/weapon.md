# Conscious Repeating Rifle

*Ranged Weapon · Two-Handed · Arcane Item*

| Damage | Range      | RoF | AP | Shots | Weight |
| :----: | :--------: | :-: | :-: | :---: | -----: |
|  2d8   | 24/48/96   |  1  |  2  |  10   |  8 lb  |

## Notes

* **Lever-Action** — Spending a Benny allows a second shot as a free action.
* **Reload** — 1 action to reload a single round, or 2 actions to reload from empty.
* Counts as a **Medium Range** weapon; –2 to Shooting rolls at Long range.

## Sentience

The rifle has a **Smarts d6** and communicates through vibrations in the stock — a slow pulse for approval, a sharp knock for disapproval. It has the **Loyal** Hindrance and refuses to fire at targets it considers allies.

* **Backseat Shooter** — Once per round the rifle may warn its wielder of a flanking enemy; the wielder is not Vulnerable from that attack.
* **Refuse to Fire** — If the wielder acts against the rifle's sense of loyalty, make an opposed **Spirit** roll. On a failure, the action jams (Shooting 1).

## Cost & Availability

**Cost:** Unique · **Availability:** Rare artifact; requires attunement
